VoltRide — Subscription Plans
==============================

HOURLY PLAN
  - Rate: ₹49 per hour
  - No commitment required
  - Battery swap fee: ₹25 per swap
  - Best for occasional riders and tourists

DAILY PLAN
  - Rate: ₹299 per day (24 hours)
  - Unlimited battery swaps included
  - Helmet included
  - Insurance coverage: basic third-party

MONTHLY PLAN
  - Rate: ₹3,499 per month
  - Unlimited battery swaps included
  - Priority scooter reservation
  - Full insurance coverage (third-party + own damage)
  - Free roadside assistance
  - Dedicated customer support line

CORPORATE PLAN
  - Custom pricing based on fleet size
  - Minimum 10 scooters
  - Dedicated account manager
  - Monthly usage reports and analytics
  - Volume discounts available

GENERAL TERMS
  - All plans require a refundable security deposit of ₹500.
  - Valid government-issued ID and driving licence required.
  - Minimum rider age: 18 years.
  - Late return fee: ₹100 per hour beyond the booked duration.
  - Cancellation within 1 hour of booking: full refund.
  - Cancellation after 1 hour: 50% refund.
