VoltRide — Battery Replacement Policy
======================================

1. REPLACEMENT CRITERIA
   - Any scooter battery that drops below 15% charge must be swapped immediately.
   - Batteries showing signs of swelling, leaking, or physical damage must be retired regardless of charge level.
   - Batteries that fail to hold above 50% charge after a full cycle are flagged for replacement.

2. SWAP PROCEDURE
   - Riders may swap batteries at any designated VoltRide battery station.
   - Station staff will scan the old battery barcode and issue a fresh one.
   - Average swap time target: under 2 minutes.

3. BATTERY LIFESPAN
   - Expected lifespan: 500 full charge cycles (~18 months of typical use).
   - After 400 cycles, batteries enter "watch" status and are inspected monthly.

4. COST
   - Battery swaps are included in all subscription plans at no additional cost.
   - Riders on hourly plans are charged ₹25 per swap.

5. DISPOSAL
   - Retired batteries are sent to our certified recycling partner, GreenCell Ltd.
   - VoltRide maintains a zero-landfill policy for all lithium-ion cells.
