VoltRide — Safety Guidelines
=============================

BEFORE EVERY RIDE
  - Check tyre pressure visually.
  - Ensure mirrors are adjusted.
  - Confirm battery is above 20%.
  - Test brakes before entering traffic.

RIDING RULES
  - Maximum speed limit: 25 km/h in city zones, 40 km/h on highways.
  - Helmets are mandatory at all times.
  - No riding on footpaths or pedestrian zones.
  - Headlights must be on after 6 PM.
  - Two riders maximum per scooter.

PARKING
  - Park only in designated VoltRide zones or public two-wheeler parking.
  - Never block entrances, exits, or wheelchair ramps.
  - Kickstand must be deployed on inclines.

ACCIDENTS & EMERGENCIES
  - Call VoltRide emergency line: 1800-VOLT-HELP (1800-8658-4357).
  - Do not move the scooter if there are injuries.
  - Take photos of the scene and exchange details with other parties.
  - File a report within 24 hours via the app or by calling support.

MAINTENANCE REPORTING
  - If you notice unusual sounds, vibrations, or brake issues, end the ride immediately.
  - Report the issue via the app → "Report a Problem" section.
  - Faulty scooters are automatically taken offline until inspected.
